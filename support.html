FAQ

1. How do I import a slip?

Open SlipDee, choose the scan or import option, and select a Thai bank payment slip screenshot from your device.

2. Does SlipDee read my SMS?

No. SlipDee does not read SMS messages.

3. Does SlipDee connect to my bank account?

No. SlipDee does not directly connect to bank accounts and does not perform banking transactions.

4. Can I export my data?

SlipDee is planned to support CSV export so users can use their expense records in spreadsheets or accounting workflows.

5. Is SlipDee available on the App Store?

SlipDee is currently being prepared for release. App Store availability will be announced when ready.
