SlipDee policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

SlipDee is designed to help users record income and expenses from Thai bank payment slip images. We believe financial records are personal and should remain under the user's control.

1. User-selected images only

SlipDee processes only the slip images that users choose to import into the app. The app does not automatically scan your photo library without your action.

2. No SMS reading

SlipDee does not read SMS messages. The app is not designed to access private text messages or bank notification messages.

3. No direct bank account access

SlipDee does not connect directly to your bank account. The app does not perform banking transactions, transfers, withdrawals, or account balance checks.

4. Slip image processing

SlipDee may use OCR-style processing to detect information such as amount, date, receiver, bank name, reference number, and category. Where possible, processing is designed to happen on the user's device.

5. No selling of personal data

SlipDee does not sell personal data to third parties.

6. User control

Users choose which slip images to import and can review or edit detected information before saving records.
