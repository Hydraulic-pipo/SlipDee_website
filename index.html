Privacy first

Your records stay under your control.

SlipDee is a tracking app, not a bank, payment processor, trading app, or investment tool. The product is designed around manual user choice and straightforward privacy boundaries.

User-selected images only

You choose which bank slip screenshots to import each time.

No SMS reading

SlipDee is not designed to access your text messages or bank alerts.

No bank account connection

The app does not connect directly to bank accounts or perform transactions.

No personal data selling

Your personal financial records are not a product to be sold.
